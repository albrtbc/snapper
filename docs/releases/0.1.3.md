## Show new cards before Snap.fan has their art

New cards such as Mordred appeared as a blank 0/0 card in the opponent panel,
because Snap.fan only had a placeholder image for them. Snapper now recognizes
those placeholders and shows the card's name, cost and power instead. When Snap.fan
publishes the real art, the card switches to it automatically.

Snapper also checks cached art again, after 7 days or after 6 hours while a card
still has only a placeholder, so replaced images no longer stay out of date.

Snapper can now update its whole card pool at once:

- **Settings** has a new **Update card pool** button. It reads Snap.fan's list of
  released and upcoming cards, downloads new and changed art, and tells you which
  cards still wait for their art. Panels show the new art without a restart.
- When images are on, Snapper runs the same update in the background shortly after
  launch if no update has run in the past week, including the first launch.
- Developers can run `npm run cards:update` to do it from a terminal.

The update reads two public list pages, which contain no card ids from your games
and no account data.

Live tracking supports Marvel Snap 58.15.1 on Linux/Proton.

Download the new AppImage and choose **Install** to update. Settings and artwork
caches are preserved. The Linux x86-64 package still requires Python 3 and an
X11/XWayland desktop.
