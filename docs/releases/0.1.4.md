## Keep transformed cards in their place

When a card you had played was transformed, for example by Sersi, Snapper put
it back in color as if it were still in your deck, so the overlay showed more
cards left than your deck held. It also dropped an opponent card from their
panel when it transformed.

Snapper now keeps every card it has seen:

- A transformed card from your deck stays in its slot, grayed out, and the card
  it became appears under **Added**.
- A transformed opponent card stays in their panel next to what it became.
- The history shows the change, such as "Baron Zemo → Proxima Midnight".

Live tracking supports Marvel Snap 58.15.1 on Linux/Proton.

Download the new AppImage and choose **Install** to update. Settings and artwork
caches are preserved. The Linux x86-64 package still requires Python 3 and an
X11/XWayland desktop.
